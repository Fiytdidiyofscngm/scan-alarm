Wake-Up Scan Alarm

The alarm keeps ringing until you scan your wake-up object.

1. Wake-up object

Keep the object far from your bed. For a photo, take 2 clear pictures of it from slightly different angles, in good light, filling most of the frame.

2. Alarm time

Keep this page open, the phone plugged in, and the volume up. The screen is kept awake while an alarm is set.
